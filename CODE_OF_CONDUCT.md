## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, respectful, and welcoming, including to people who disagree with you.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including continuing to contact someone who has asked you to stop.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, security advisories, release notes and comments, the command line's package page on GitHub Packages, the VS Code extension's pages (and its listing on any extension marketplace), the documentation website, and the project's chat or social channels — and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere, such as at an event or in a support conversation.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain why when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@techieutils](https://github.com/techieutils). If the report involves the maintainer, or you would rather not write to them on GitHub, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/techieutils/api-workbench/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct report" — only the maintainers can see it.

Include what happened, where (links help), when, and who was involved, and anything else you think matters. You do not need proof, and you do not need to have been the person affected.

Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly: maintainers aim to acknowledge it within a week and to tell you what they decided. They keep the reporter's identity and the details private, share them only with those who need them to handle the report, and will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

## Enforcement

Following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), maintainers decide on one of these, by how serious the behaviour was:

1. **Correction** — for inappropriate language or other unprofessional behaviour: a private, written explanation of why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — for a single serious incident or repeated behaviour: a warning with consequences for continuing, and no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour: no interaction or public communication with the community for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people: no public interaction within the community.

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement decision was wrong, reply to the maintainer who contacted you, or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/techieutils/api-workbench/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct appeal", within 30 days. Say what you think was mistaken and why. A maintainer who was not part of the original decision reviews the appeal where possible, and the outcome is final.

## Conflicts of interest

A maintainer who is involved in a report — as the reporter, the person reported, or someone close to either — takes no part in handling it.
